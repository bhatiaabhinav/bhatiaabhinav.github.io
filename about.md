## About

Hello, I'm an **Applied Scientist** at **Microsoft Xbox Game Studios** in **Redmond**, where I work on developing AI for automated game-playing.

I have a **Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Massachusetts, Amherst**, advised by **[Prof. Shlomo Zilberstein](https://groups.cs.umass.edu/shlomo/)**. My Ph.D. research broadly focused on **reinforcement learning**, **real-time planning**, and **metareasoning for autonomous decision-making under constraints** such as time, data and safety; specific focus areas included **safe-RL**, **offline-RL**, **meta-RL**, **robotics** and **autonomous vehicles**.

Before my Ph.D., I was a research engineer at **Singapore Management University**, working with [Prof. Pradeep Varakantham](http://www.mysmu.edu/faculty/pradeepv/) and [Prof. Akshat Kumar](http://www.mysmu.edu/faculty/akshatkumar/index.html) on AI-driven solutions for large-scale constrained resource allocation. My work experience also includes an internship at **Microsoft Xbox Game Studios**, where I worked on developing game-playing AI; a software engineering position at **Walmart e-commerce**; and an internship at **Amazon**, working on Prime Video. I completed my **B.E. (Hons.) in Computer Science** from **BITS-Pilani, India**, in 2015.
